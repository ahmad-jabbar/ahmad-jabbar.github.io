Ahmad Jabbar

jabbar@stanford.edu

I am a PhD candidate at Stanford Linguistics and a member of the Stanford NLP Group. I work on Semantics, Computational Linguistics, and Philosophy of Language, often interfacing with Logic, Epistemology, Syntax, and Cognitive Psychology. My dissertation committee is: Christopher Potts (chair), Cleo Condoravdi (chair), and Thomas Icard. As an undergrad, I read Philosophy.

With Jasper Jian, I co-organize a group at the intersection of language modeling and linguistic theory. With Cleo Condoravdi, I co-organized the Construction of Meaning workshop in 2024-25.

If you're an undergrad or high school student (anywhere in the world) and interested in any of the topics I work on, feel free to reach out.
